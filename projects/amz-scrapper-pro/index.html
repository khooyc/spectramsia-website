How it works

Stay on the Amazon page. Leave with structured data.

A simple three-step flow — no manual copy-paste.

Step 1

Open a supported Amazon page

Visit a product page or search results page on one of eight supported Amazon marketplaces, then open the extension.

Step 2

Select products and fields

Rescan the page, select the listings you want with the orange highlight confirming each pick, and choose exactly which data points to extract.

Step 3

Export Excel or CSV

Run the extraction and download the completed table as a formatted workbook, a CSV, or copy it straight to your clipboard.

Installation

Install the extension in about five minutes

AMZ Scrapper Pro is currently distributed through GitHub — there's no Chrome Web Store listing yet. Keep the extracted folder after installation; your browser loads the extension from that location.

FAQ

Questions before you install

Straight answers about what the extension can — and cannot — do.

Which Amazon sites are supported?

Amazon US, UK, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Canada and Australia are included in version 2.1.

Does it work on search results and product pages?

Yes. On search pages you can choose multiple products; on product pages the extension can inspect the current listing and its available variants.

What can I export?

You can download a formatted Excel workbook or a UTF-8 CSV file. A copy-to-clipboard option is also available.

Is it affiliated with Amazon?

No. AMZ Scrapper Pro is an independent productivity tool and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Amazon.

Is automated extraction always permitted?

You are responsible for using the extension in accordance with Amazon's Conditions of Use and any laws or policies that apply in your jurisdiction.

Where can I report a problem?

Open a GitHub issue with steps to reproduce it. Never include account credentials, customer information, or other private data.
