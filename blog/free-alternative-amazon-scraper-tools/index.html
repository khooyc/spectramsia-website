Free Alternatives to Paid Amazon Scraper Tools

By Khoo YC · Spectra · Updated 17 August 2026
Quick answer

If the job is pulling data off Amazon pages you're already viewing — price, ASIN, ratings, BSR, variants — into Excel or CSV, a free open-source extension like AMZ Scrapper Pro covers it completely. Paid all-in-one research suites earn their subscription with market-wide keyword volume and cross-catalog trend data on top — which you only need if that's actually the job.

Most "Amazon scraper tool" searches lead to paid subscription suites built for keyword research, sales estimation, and PPC management across an entire category. That's real, valuable functionality — and it costs a recurring fee because it requires hosted databases and ongoing crawling infrastructure most sellers don't need for a simple task: getting the data off a page you're already looking at into a spreadsheet.

When you actually need the paid tier

  • You're doing keyword research across thousands of search terms you haven't visited
  • You need historical sales trend estimates for products you don't own
  • You want automated PPC bid management

None of that is what a page-level scraper does — so paying for it just to export product data is usually overkill.

When the free tool is enough

  • Competitor price checks on a specific list of ASINs
  • Catalog audits of your own listings
  • One-off market snapshots for a pitch or report
  • Tracking BSR and variant pricing on products you already follow

FAQ

Can a free tool really replace a paid suite?

For page-level data export, yes. For market-wide keyword and trend analysis, no — that's a different job requiring hosted infrastructure a free browser extension can't do.

Is AMZ Scrapper Pro open source?

Yes, MIT licensed on GitHub — read every line before you install it.

What's the catch?

No cost catch, but also no dedicated support line and no hosted keyword database — updates depend on the maintainer, not a company roadmap.
